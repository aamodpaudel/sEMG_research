How it works

The page tells Nullpoint what you can say.

  1. The DOM Model numbers the page.Every link, button and field gets a number and a short spoken name.
  2. You look, then mouth a command."Click sign in", "click four", "scroll down". Your gaze picks the area.
  3. The sEMG Model listens for only those words.A handful of choices instead of a dictionary. That is what makes a small model accurate.

Who it is for

Built for access. Useful to everyone.

First

People who cannot use their hands or voice.

  • Voice loss after laryngectomy: the mouth still forms every word.
  • Spinal cord injury: face and eyes replace hands.
  • Anyone for whom a keyboard is a barrier, not a tool.
Then

Everyone whose fingers are tired.

  • Give your hands a rest after a day of typing and clicking.
  • Type silently in a library, on a train, in a meeting.
  • Keep working when your hands are busy.
